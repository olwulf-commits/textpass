# Privacy Notice for TextPass

Effective: September 30, 2026

[Deutsche Fassung](PRIVACY.md)

TextPass by Olaf Wulf consists of instructions for drafting and revising text. The plugin does not operate its own server, database, or any analytics or tracking services.

By using the plugin, Olaf Wulf does not gain access to any text you enter or to your personal data. The package itself does not store user input and does not independently transmit it to the publisher.

The AI application used processes input and output in accordance with the terms and privacy policies of its respective provider. TextPass does not alter this processing or the retention periods applicable there.

If the optional external reference tools are used, search terms may be transmitted to these services by the AI application. Personal information and confidential text passages should not be included in such requests.

When contacting us voluntarily or making public GitHub posts, the publisher receives the information provided there. Please do not publish any confidential texts or personal data of third parties there.
